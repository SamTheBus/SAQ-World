New melee mobs

Lava Serpent, Hell Bat, Slag Slime, Swamp Basilisk, Marsh Ghost and Corroded Golem each have a distinct windup, strike and recovery. Their painters retain Dungeon’s designs with World scale, direction and contact adjustments.

How shots work

Casters warn with their body, then fire along the direction committed at the start of the warning. A moving player can dodge. Shots travel through space and stop at solid walls, closed doorways or the first eligible player. They use the existing damage, level-gap and defense rules. Projectile colors do not add unimplemented status effects.

Ranged mobs occupy a minority of each eligible pack or floor. The update changes species diversity without increasing the established enemy counts or active actor cap.

Your existing campaign

Continue your usual save. The original seven definitions and their profile IDs remain intact. Eligible untouched encounters adopt a deterministic mixture with the same actor IDs, counts, locations and floor layouts. Fought, wounded, cleared or rewarded encounters retain their current episode. Their later legal renewal is separate from this update.

Remaining roster

Fourteen of Dungeon’s ordinary families remain for later batches: the rare mountain creatures and the remaining Void, Temporal and Cyber pools. Bosses, chest specials and boss-summoned hazards are outside this batch. Physical-phone performance and online multiplayer have not been verified by this pass.
