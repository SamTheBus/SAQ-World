# SAQ World art direction

Cel shading remains the intended direction for the world and every mob.

The blanket renderer shading and terrain posterization were rejected by Sammy.
They have been removed. The previous terrain detail and player appearance are
restored. The five creature redesigns and their front attack anatomy remain.

- Shape shadows around each creature or object's actual form and material.
- Keep accepted terrain detail, texture, color variation, and readable surfaces.
- Use restrained highlights and intentional dark outlines at gameplay scale.
- Preserve solid body masses and distinct silhouettes for every mob family.
- Keep supporting legs planted while the separate front anatomy attacks.
- Avoid generic diagonal bands across unrelated shapes or the entire scene.
- Avoid automatic palette reduction of terrain and scenery.
- Review a small representative in-game sample before applying another style
  treatment across the roster or world.

A complete authored cel-shading pass across all mobs and the world remains
unfinished. Restoring the rejected treatment does not complete that art work.
